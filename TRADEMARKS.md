The **name "Apex Dash"**, the **shift-light logo and app icon**, and the
**apexdash.pro** domain are not covered by that licence. They identify the
official app published by Bekir Ersever, so that people know what they are
installing and who stands behind it.

## You may

- Say that your project is based on, forked from or compatible with Apex Dash.
- Use the name in articles, reviews, videos and tutorials about the app.
- Keep the name and icon in a fork you build and run for yourself.

## You may not, without written permission

- Publish an app, in any app store, under the name Apex Dash or a confusingly
  similar name.
- Use the Apex Dash logo or app icon, or a close imitation of them, for a
  different build or product.
- Suggest that your build is the official app or endorsed by this project.

If you publish a fork, give it its own name and icon, and point to this
repository as the original.

Report misuse by opening an issue.
